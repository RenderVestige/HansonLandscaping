Welcome to Hanson's Landscaping


We at Hanson's Landscaping are committed to providing our customers with the very best in landscaping services. Combining our personal passion for landscape design with your needs and desires, we will work with you to create your own beautiful outdoor environment.

Hanson’s Landscaping was established in 1999 by Mark and Anne Hanson. Over the years, the company has grown and matured into well respected, local contractor with a dedication to quality. After celebrating 15 years in the business, Hanson’s Landscaping is now operated by Mark and Anne’s son, Joe Hanson and his wife Alison.

Joe brings fresh and exciting ideas to the company, while still maintaining the same level of service and industry quality standards. Joe is educated in civil engineering and holds a professional engineer license in the state of Wisconsin.

Hanson’s Landscaping has a core veteran group of dedicated staff, who strive to bring exceptional landscape design, construction, and management services to the greater Milwaukee/Waukesha area. From the planning stage to the finished product, Hanson's will devote their attention to every detail of your project.

Hanson's Landscaping is a member of the Metropolitan Builders' Association.
Hanson's Landscaping is a Better Business Bureau member, with an A+ rating.
Hanson's Landscaping is also a member of the Wisconsin Landscape Contractors Association.
